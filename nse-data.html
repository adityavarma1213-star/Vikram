How acquisition actually works

VIKRAM's real acquisition mechanism (server/src/ingest.js) fetches Cash Market and Futures & Options end-of-day bhavcopy files directly from nsearchives.nseindia.com, one NSE trading day at a time. It searches backward from today for the most recent day not yet stored (up to 10 calendar days), ingests both CM and F&O for that day, then re-materializes scanner results. It runs on a schedule server-side (see render.yaml's vikram-eod-ingestion cron job) and requires a live Postgres database.

What date range can actually be acquired: only the most recent not-yet-stored trading day, one at a time. There is currently no bulk/arbitrary historical date-range acquisition (e.g. "fetch Jan 2022–Dec 2023") anywhere in this codebase, on the website or on the backend — building that would be a separate, larger project.

Environment & Authorization

Acquisition requires: DATABASE_URL (Postgres connection), AUTH_SECRET (≥16 chars, session token signing — see server/src/auth.js), and ADMIN_EMAILS (comma-separated allowlist — see server/src/adminAuth.js). None of these are exposed to frontend JavaScript; the admin check happens entirely on the server by looking up the signed-in user's email from the database, never from anything the browser sends. If ADMIN_EMAILS is unset, acquisition is unavailable to everyone (fails closed), even a successfully logged-in user.
